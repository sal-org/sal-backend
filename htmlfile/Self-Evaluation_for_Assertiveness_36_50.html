About the Report:

What is Assertiveness?

Assertiveness is the ability to express your thoughts, feelings, needs, and rights openly and honestly while respecting the rights of others. It is a balanced style of communication that avoids two extremes:

  • Passive behaviour – staying quiet, avoiding conflict, or allowing others to impose on you, often leading to frustration or resentment.
  • Aggressive behaviour – expressing yourself in a forceful, hostile, or disrespectful way, which may harm relationships and create tension.

What is your Assertiveness Scale?

The Assertiveness Scale is a self-evaluation tool designed to measure how confidently and respectfully you express yourself in everyday situations. It reflects your ability to communicate openly, set boundaries, handle conflict, and stand up for your rights without being passive or aggressive.

This scale is not a diagnostic test but a reflection tool to:

  • Increase awareness of your current communication style.
  • Identify strengths and areas for growth.
  • Serve as a starting point for practicing and developing assertiveness skills.
