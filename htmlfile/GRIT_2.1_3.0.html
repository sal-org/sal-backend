About the Report:

What is GRIT?

  • Grit is defined as perseverance and passion for long-term goals
  • It entails working strenuously toward challenges, maintaining effort and interest over years despite failure, adversity, and plateaus in progress
  • Grit is unrelated to talent and can be built through a growth mindset

Grit is a multidimensional construct including perseverance of effort and consistency of interests as its dimensions. In essence grit refers to determination to achieve long term goals of life despite of unfavourable and unavoidable circumstances.

What is your GRIT Scale?


This scale is designed to help individuals understand how much grit they possess, which is associated with achieving success in various fields, such as education, career, and personal development.


This interpretation of the grit score is aimed at helping individuals understand where they stand in terms of their perseverance and passion for long-term goals. By using this awareness, you can focus on strategies to increase your grit, whether you're trying to stay focused on personal projects, career goals, or other areas of life.
