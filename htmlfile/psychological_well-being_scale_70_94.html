About the Report:

The Psychological Well-Being Scale (PWB-18), developed by Carol Ryff, measures the core aspects of positive functioning and emotional wellness. It is designed to give a broad, holistic picture of an individual’s psychological well-being. It helps users understand how they are doing overall and identify areas where they may benefit from support, reflection, or self-care.

Ryff operationalized psychological wellbeing as a six-dimensional model of human characteristics: self-acceptance, positive relations with others, environmental mastery, personal growth, autonomy, and purpose in life.
